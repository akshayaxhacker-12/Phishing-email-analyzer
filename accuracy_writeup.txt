6. TUNING NOTES

The analyzer uses weighted indicators such as display-name spoofing,
Reply-To mismatch, SPF/DKIM/DMARC failures, malicious URLs, and
malicious attachments.

The risk score is capped at 100.

Verdict thresholds:
- Low: 0-29
- Medium: 30-59
- High: 60-79
- Critical: 80-100

The current results are based on a small synthetic test dataset.
Therefore, the observed 0% false-positive and 0% false-negative rates
should not be treated as a production accuracy measurement.

Further testing with a larger set of sanitized or mentor-approved
phishing and legitimate emails is recommended.


7. DATA SAFETY CONFIRMATION

Only sanitized synthetic test email samples were used during development
and testing. No real employee or customer emails were processed.
Email attachments were analyzed only through metadata and SHA-256 hashes
and were not executed.
